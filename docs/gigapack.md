# GigaPack by MatoZanato 1.0 (Football Life 2027)

A big ready-made world for **Football Life 2027**: **317 leagues and 5,239 clubs**, with their kits,
crests, logos and scoreboards. One zip, installed by FL Mod Studio, no Build needed. Free.

## Install, step by step

In this order, don't skip one.

**Before you start:** Football Life 2027 with **update 1.1**, on Windows. Close the game and its launcher.

1. **Mod Studio.** Right-click `FL26ModStudio-0.4.0.zip` → **Extract All...** → a folder of its own
   (for example `Documents\FL26 Mod Studio`). Don't run it from inside the zip, don't move
   `FL26ModStudio.exe` out of its folder. Double-click `FL26ModStudio.exe`. If Windows says
   "Windows protected your PC": **More info** → **Run anyway**.
2. **Your game folder.** In Mod Studio: **5. Settings** → **Browse...** → the folder with
   `FL_2027.exe` (not `SiderAddons`) → **Use this folder**. You should see `Found: FL_2027.exe`.
   The first time it unpacks the game's tables: a minute or two, wait.
3. **GigaPack.** Don't unzip it. **1. Home** → **Import a world zip** → pick `GigaPack-1.0.zip` →
   **Install the world**. Had a GigaPack beta in? It asks to replace it: **Yes** (your saves are not
   touched). Wait for **Ready to play**. No Build needed. If it says **Almost ready**, it lists what
   to fix. Mod Studio can walk you through this too: **Help** → **Install a Modpack, step by step**.
4. **Play.** Green **Start the game** in Mod Studio (or FL27's launcher, not `FL_2027.exe`
   directly) → **Master League** → **New game**. At **Select Team** the new leagues are under their
   countries.

**Back to the plain game:** **Game** → **Put Sider back as before Mod Studio...** Nothing is deleted.
Your careers stay with the world they were made in.

**Updating from a GigaPack beta?** Same steps with the new Mod Studio and the new zip, then a new
Master League.

More detail: [Install a ready pack](../guides/en/install-a-pack.md).

## What GigaPack can't do (yet)

- FL27 only: Mod Studio won't put it into Football Life 2026.
- **No SmokePatch face pack.** GigaPack doesn't include SmokePatch's face pack (real player faces):
  that is SmokePatch's work, not ours. If you want it, get it from SmokePatch and install it
  separately.
- Only a NEW Master League gets the new leagues. A career you started before (also on a GigaPack
  beta) doesn't.
- Real kits for 600 clubs in 58 leagues ([the list](gigapack-real-kits-1.0.md)). Every other club
  wears a kit Mod Studio draws in its colours, and on those the player's name sits under the
  number (fixed in the next version).
- Loading takes longer than in the plain game (a new career about a minute more, each day a few
  seconds more).
- The Mod Studio window can freeze for about 2 s (Check the plan or the start of a Build when Mod
  Studio is in another language, the first read of a big zip), then goes on. Don't close it.

## Known issues

- In another language, two status lines of Make it editable stay in English.
- FL27, Become a Legend in a very big world: one report of an empty league Calendar and another
  competition's result screen (not seen in our careers).
- FL27: the two legs of the 3rd European qualifying round are five days apart (Thursday, then
  Tuesday): a full week doesn't fit before FL27's league phase.
- FL27: in the AFC Champions League group stage two clubs from the same country can still land in
  one group (seen once in our test season, 1 of 8 groups).
- After Make it editable + Build, an updated world lists thousands of untouched files as "added or
  changed" (your own files are kept).
- After an Update, a club you edited can leave its old kit folder ("... EDIT") behind.

All of Mod Studio's known issues: [known issues](known-issues.md).

## Tested

**Tested in game on this exact build** with GigaPack 1.0: a new Master League in Poland through two
season ends. European competitions, promotion and relegation and the season calendar work.

**Not confirmed on this build** (automatic tests or an older build only; tell us what you see):

- Kick Off with our leagues: kits to pick, the league's scoreboard and badge
- league cup and third cup on the days set in My world
- European play-off and league phase dates
- club budgets, fans, TV money and sponsors from the pack
- numbers and names on drawn kits, varied boots and gloves
- light and dark competition logos, the trophy ceremony with the real trophy
- Select Team: every new club has its own squad; no player twice in the world
- League Info "Lower League" for a league in the middle of a pyramid
- leagues without 20 clubs: number of matchdays
- Cup mode with our cups, Edit mode save + restart
- a club without a kit while Kit Server is on
- switching careers between worlds (last checked on beta 4)
