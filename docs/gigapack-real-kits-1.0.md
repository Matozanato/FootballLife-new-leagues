# GigaPack 1.0: clubs with real kits

**600 clubs in 58 leagues** wear real kits made by kit-pack authors (not all from the same season). Every other club wears a kit Mod Studio draws in its colours, with its crest. More real kits come in updates.

Made a kit for a club that isn't here? Send it in **#gigapack-submissions** on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/StQqtk3G3M).
